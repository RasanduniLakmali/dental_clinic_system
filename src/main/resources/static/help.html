Help & Guidance

Step-by-step guidance for Sunrise Dental Clinic staff.

🔐 Signing In

Use the username and password provided by the clinic administrator.

If you forget your password, contact an Admin to reset your account.

📅 Registering a New Appointment

  1. Open Register Appointment from the side menu.
  2. Enter the patient's full name, address and contact number.
  3. Select the dentist and treatment type.
  4. Select the appointment date and time.
  5. Click Save Appointment.
  6. The system automatically generates an appointment number.

🔎 Finding an Appointment

  1. Open Search & Bill.
  2. Enter the appointment number.
  3. Click Find.
  4. The patient's appointment details will appear.

🧾 Generating a Bill

  1. Search for the appointment.
  2. Click Calculate Bill.
  3. The system calculates the consultation fee and treatment fee.
  4. Review the total amount.
  5. Click Print or Download PDF.

👥 Managing Staff

Admin users can create Dentist and Receptionist accounts from the Manage Staff page.

A temporary password is generated automatically when a new account is created.

🚪 Signing Out

Click Log out in the side navigation when you finish using the system.

Always sign out when using a shared reception computer.
